## B82 — shared Yharnam Together network-link CPU optimization (2026-11-11)

- [B82 real shared client/server code](ULTIMATE_BATCH_82.md): `GameLinkPeer::Flush` previously compared each due reliable resend to *every* already queued fresh entry with `std::ranges::none_of`. When a stalled/rejoining member holds thousands of fresh messages and up to 64 due resends, this introduces unnecessary quadratic membership work. B82 keeps the exact packets/sequence/ACK/backoff/capped-resends, and uses an expected O(1) `std::unordered_set<u32>` lookup to ensure no reliable resend is emitted twice.

Real shared `server/coop/game_link.h` and emulator `bloodborne_game_link.h`; no client/server protocol change.
